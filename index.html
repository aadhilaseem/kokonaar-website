From husk to product

How coconut husk becomes cocopeat

Every block goes through the same careful preparation before it is compressed and packed.

  1. Collect

    Coconut husk

    The fibrous outer husk of the coconut is the raw material for every product.

  2. Wash & dry

    Washed and sun-dried

    Washing lowers salts, and drying prepares the material for sieving.

  3. Sieve

    Sieved to grade

    Sieving gives an even texture with good aeration and drainage.

  4. Compress

    Compressed and packed

    Pressed into blocks or briquettes, or packed loose for bulk orders.
